# Tricon CoCo Lab — start here

You only do three things yourself:

1. Open this folder in CoCo Desktop (**File → Open Folder**) and click **Trust**.
2. Make sure the connection at the top shows role **DEMO_ROLE**.
3. Paste the prompts from the lab guide into the CoCo chat panel.

CoCo does everything else: it writes the files, runs dbt **inside Snowflake**
(nothing to install), and checks its own work.

Your schema: **DEMO_DB.TPCH_<INITIALS>**

This follows Jeremiah Hansen's *Data Engineering with CoCo* guide. Finished answers for each module are in `solutions/`.

Stuck? Paste the module's **Catch me up** prompt, or raise a hand.
